# Awesome-Civic-Tech-Open-Government-Data-Analytics

# Top Civic Tech & Open Government Data Analytics Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS/Hosted Platforms & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Open Data Portals, Participatory Democracy, Public Budgeting, FOIA/Transparency Requests, Civic GIS & Government Analytics*  
**Last updated: November 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS/hosted platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Civic Tech & Open Government Data Analytics**. These tools publish, catalog, visualize, audit, and analyze public datasets, municipal budgets, legislative proceedings, Freedom of Information (FOIA) requests, and citizen engagement workflows across municipal portals, national open-data hubs, and civic research platforms.

**Examples** include OpenGov, Tyler Technologies, Accela, Opendatasoft, Socrata (Tyler Data & Insights), Granicus, SeamlessDocs (GovOS), Polco, Zencity, GovQA, and eyalgolan/statecraft-israel (the category leaders and notable community entries). 

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosted open-data catalogs (`CKAN`, `DKAN`, `uData`), participatory democracy & citizen engagement (`Decidim`, `Consul Democracy`), FOIA/right-to-information portals (`Alaveteli`, `Froide`), public budget transparency (`OpenSpending`), and civic analytics (`eyalgolan/statecraft-israel`, `Datasette`) — ideal for civic technologists, policy researchers, data journalists, municipal IT teams, and open-government advocates.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[OpenGov](https://opengov.com)**  
  Cloud ERP, budgeting, performance analytics, and open-data transparency suite built specifically for local and state governments.

- **[Tyler Technologies & Socrata (Data & Insights)](https://tylertech.com)**  
  Leading public-sector software and cloud open-data platform powering municipal transparency portals, civic APIs, and cross-agency data analytics.

- **[Opendatasoft](https://opendatasoft.com)**  
  Cloud data-sharing and open-government portal platform enabling municipalities and public agencies to publish, visualize, and distribute interactive datasets and APIs.

- **[Accela](https://accela.com)**  
  Cloud civic engagement and regulatory management platform streamlining municipal permitting, licensing, code enforcement, and public service workflows.

- **[Granicus](https://granicus.com)**  
  Cloud government experience platform combining legislative agenda management, public meeting streaming, citizen communications, and FOIA/records workflows.

- **[Zencity](https://zencity.io)**  
  AI-powered community engagement and civic sentiment analytics platform aggregating resident feedback across surveys, social channels, and 311 data.

- **[Polco](https://polco.us)**  
  Civic engagement and community performance analytics platform connecting local governments with verified resident polling and national benchmarking datasets.

- **[GovQA (Granicus)](https://govqa.com)**  
  Cloud public records request (FOIA) management, redaction, and compliance workflow automation platform for government agencies.

- **[SeamlessDocs (GovOS)](https://govos.com)**  
  Cloud government forms, digital signatures, and citizen self-service workflow platform modernizing paper-based municipal processes.


## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[eyalgolan/statecraft-israel](https://github.com)**  
  Open-source civic tech and statecraft/public-policy simulation and data analysis project exploring governance, civic systems, and public data modeling.

- **[CKAN (`ckan/ckan`)](https://github.com)**  
  World's leading open-source data management system powering national and municipal open-data portals (`data.gov`, `data.overheid.nl`, `open.canada.ca`) with rich metadata, spatial search, and APIs.

- **[Decidim (`decidim/decidim`)](https://github.com)**  
  Flagship open-source participatory democracy framework built in Ruby on Rails for citizen assemblies, participatory budgeting, consultations, and public proposals.

- **[Consul Democracy (`consuldemocracy/consuldemocracy`)](https://github.com)**  
  Widely deployed open-source citizen participation and open-government platform supporting debates, proposals, participatory budgeting, and collaborative legislation.

- **[Alaveteli (`mysociety/alaveteli`)](https://github.com)**  
  Open-source Freedom of Information (FOIA) request platform developed by mySociety (powering WhatDoTheyKnow) that automates public records requests and archives responses publicly.

- **[Froide (`okfde/froide`)](https://github.com)**  
  Modern Python/Django open-source Freedom of Information portal developed by Open Knowledge Foundation Germany (powering FragDenStaat) with automated redaction and tracking.

- **[DKAN (`GetDKAN/dkan`)](https://github.com)**  
  Open-source Drupal-based open-data cataloging, visualization, and DCAT-compliant API platform engineered for government agencies and civic institutions.

- **[FixMyStreet (`mysociety/fixmystreet`)](https://github.com)**  
  Open-source map-based civic issue reporting platform enabling residents to report, view, and track local street, lighting, and infrastructure problems directly with councils.

- **[Datasette (`simonw/datasette`)](https://github.com)**  
  Open-source tool for exploring, querying, and publishing public SQLite datasets as instant interactive web portals and JSON APIs—widely used in data journalism and civic tech.

- **[Open311 / Libre311 (`Libre311/Libre311`)](https://github.com)**  
  Open-source implementation of the Open311 civic issue-tracking standard providing mobile-friendly resident reporting and municipal service-request routing.

- **[uData (`opendatateam/udata`)](https://github.com)**  
  Customizable open-source social open-data portal engine powering `data.gouv.fr`, enabling community dataset contributions, reuses, and federation.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **[OpenSpending (`openspending/openspending`)](https://github.com)** for open-source fiscal transparency, public budget visualization, and government spending analytics.
- **[TerriaJS (`TerriaJS/terriajs`)](https://github.com)** and **[QGIS (`qgis/QGIS`)](https://github.com)** for open-source 3D/2D geospatial open-data catalogs, urban planning maps, and civic GIS analytics.
- **[Apache Superset (`apache/superset`)](https://github.com)** and **[Metabase (`metabase/metabase`)](https://github.com)** for self-hosted public transparency dashboards and cross-departmental civic BI.
- **[LiquidFeedback (`liquidfeedback`)](https://liquidfeedback.com)** and **[Pol.is (`compdemocracy/polis`)](https://github.com)** for open-source real-time consensus-finding and large-scale public opinion clustering.
- **[OpenCivicData (`opencivicdata`)](https://github.com)** for standardized open-source schemas and scrapers collecting legislative bills, votes, people, and municipal jurisdictions.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **CKAN** or **uData** for DCAT-compliant open-data catalogs, **Decidim** or **Consul Democracy** for participatory budgeting and citizen engagement, and **Datasette + TerriaJS** for interactive civic data journalism and geospatial portals.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Public sector and civic data platforms must comply with regional accessibility mandates (WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA), open-data standards (DCAT-AP), and data protection laws (GDPR, FOIA PII redaction rules).
- Always verify dataset provenance, update cadences, and open-data licenses (CC0, CC-BY, ODbL) before republishing government analytics.
